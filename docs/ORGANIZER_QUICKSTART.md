# Chabad at Lehigh — organizer quick start

One page for the people who run Chabad at Lehigh. Detailed guides are in the app under **Dashboard → Help**.

## Sign in

Open the site → **Sign in** → enter your email → tap the link in the email, or type the 6-digit code.
No password. Once an admin gives you a role, a **Dashboard** button appears at the top.

## Your dashboard

| Section | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| Overview | What needs attention: RSVPs to approve, new suggestions, and the getting-started checklist for admins |
| Events | Create, publish and duplicate events. Each event has **Details**, **Attendees & check-in**, **Menu** and **Dietary** tabs |
| Dishes, Ingredients | Your recipe library. Allergens come from the ingredients |
| Suggestions | Dish ideas from students |
| Announcements | Posts on the home page |
| People & roles | Who can do what *(admins)* |
| Settings | Contact details (including the phone shown for allergy questions), address, directions, parking *(admins)* |
| Audit log | Record of important changes *(admins)* |
| Help | Step-by-step guides |

## Every week (about 5 minutes)

1. **Events → Past** → last week's dinner → **Duplicate for next week** → check the date → **Status: Published** → **Save event**.
2. **Menu** tab → add dishes → tick the preparation confirmation → **Publish**.
3. Share it: **Copy event link** for group chats, or **Invite people** on the Attendees tab.
4. If **Overview** shows RSVPs needing approval, approve or decline them. Someone texts that they can't come?
   **Cancel RSVP** on the Attendees tab frees their seat for the waitlist.
5. On the day: kitchen prints the **Dietary** tab; at the door, tap **Check in** on **Attendees & check-in**.

## Roles

| Role | Can |
| --- | --- |
| Admin | Everything, including roles and settings. Keep at least two. |
| Organizer | Events, RSVPs, check-in, invitations, announcements, menus. Sees anonymous dietary counts. |
| Kitchen staff | Dishes, ingredients, menus, and the named dietary report (every view is logged). |

To add someone: **People & roles** → their email → pick a role. It works even before they've signed in.

## Changing or cancelling an event

Set **Status → Cancelled** (don't delete). Cancelling, or changing the time or place, sends everyone who RSVP'd an
urgent in-app notification. There are no automatic emails or texts — use **Email everyone going** for those.

## The allergy rule

Never describe food as "safe", "allergen-free" or "guaranteed". Votes and suggestions are ideas, not safety
information. Whenever ingredients or allergens change, post an **urgent** menu update — everyone who RSVP'd is notified in the app.
