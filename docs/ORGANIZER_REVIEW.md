# Organizer review and sign-off

Please go through this list with the Chabad at Lehigh organizers (and whoever prepares the food)
**before** the app is announced to students. Tick each item, note changes needed, and record
who approved it. Nothing here replaces talking directly with people who have serious allergies.

## 1. RSVP workflow

- [ ] We agree on the default for each kind of event: confirm automatically, guests/community
      members need approval, or every RSVP needs approval.
- [ ] Capacity and waitlist behaviour is right: when someone cancels, the next person on the
      waitlist is confirmed automatically and notified in the app.
- [ ] Plus-one limits per event are right (0–10). Guests' names are optional.
- [ ] We know how to approve/decline RSVPs and guests, confirm someone over capacity, and check
      people in at the door (Dashboard → Event → Attendees & check-in).
- [ ] RSVP deadlines and "RSVPs closed" are used the way we want.
- [ ] We know how to email attendees (the "Email attendees" button opens your email app with
      everyone in BCC) and export the list as a spreadsheet (no dietary data in the export).

## 2. Meals, ingredients and allergens

- [ ] Whoever cooks agrees to enter **every ingredient** for each dish, including sauces,
      dressings, oils, toppings and packaged items — and to check package labels for allergens.
- [ ] We understand the three kinds of allergen information the app shows:
      - **Contains** — comes from the ingredient list.
      - **May contain / cross-contact** — entered by the kitchen for shared equipment, fryers,
        cutting boards, serving areas, etc.
      - **Related** — the app points out related allergens, e.g. *sesame oil* vs *sesame seeds*,
        *wheat* vs *gluten*.
- [ ] Sesame seeds and sesame oil are entered as **separate** allergens wherever they are used.
- [ ] The general kitchen note (e.g. "shared kitchen that also handles sesame and tree nuts") is
      accurate for our kitchen.
- [ ] A staff member will tick the **preparation confirmation** only after checking that the
      ingredients, allergens and cross-contact notes match how the food will actually be made.
      The app will not publish a menu without it.
- [ ] We know that editing a dish later does **not** change a published menu; we publish a new
      version with a short "what changed" note.
- [ ] We know how to post an **urgent** menu update (it notifies everyone who RSVP'd) and will use
      it whenever an allergen-relevant change happens — including day-of substitutions.
- [ ] We are comfortable with the disclaimer wording shown on every menu, and with the contact
      details shown for questions (Dashboard → Settings).

## 3. Voting and suggestions

- [ ] Everyone understands that dish votes and suggestions are **advisory only**. Staff choose
      the final menu.
- [ ] **A popular dish is never evidence that it is safe for someone with an allergy.** We will not
      describe any dish as "safe", "allergen-free" or "guaranteed" based on votes or ingredient lists.

## 4. Dietary privacy

- [ ] We agree on who gets the **Kitchen staff** and **Admin** roles — these are the only people
      who can see names next to allergies (Dashboard → Event → Dietary). Keep this list short.
- [ ] Organizers without those roles see only anonymous counts. We're comfortable with that.
- [ ] Every time someone opens the named dietary report it is recorded in the audit log
      (Dashboard → Audit log, admins only).
- [ ] People can choose not to share their details with the kitchen, and can delete their
      dietary information — or their whole account — at any time from Profile & settings.
      If they opt out, we will ask them directly.
- [ ] The privacy page (`/privacy`) matches how we actually handle information.

## 5. Accounts and roles

- [ ] Lehigh email addresses automatically get the *Student* role; everyone else starts as a
      *Community guest*. The student email domain list is correct (Dashboard → Settings).
- [ ] At least **two** trusted people are admins (so access isn't lost if one is unavailable).
- [ ] We know how to give and remove roles (Dashboard → People & roles).

## 6. Before launch

- [ ] Demo content removed (`supabase/scripts/remove_demo_data.sql`).
- [ ] Contact name, email, phone, address and directions (pre-filled from public listings) confirmed or
      corrected, and parking notes added (Dashboard → Settings).
- [ ] Sign-in emails arrive reliably for a Lehigh address and a Gmail address (custom SMTP is set up).
- [ ] A dry run of one real Shabbat dinner: create event → publish menu → RSVPs → check-in.
