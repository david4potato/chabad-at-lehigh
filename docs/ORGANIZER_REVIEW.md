# Organizer review and sign-off

Go through this with the organizers and whoever cooks **before** announcing the app. Tick each item,
note changes, and record who approved. Nothing here replaces talking directly with people who have
serious allergies.

## 1. RSVPs

- [ ] Default confirmation per kind of event: automatic, guests/community need approval, or every RSVP needs approval.
- [ ] Capacity and waitlist: when someone cancels, the next person is confirmed and notified in the app.
- [ ] Plus-one limits (0–10) and RSVP deadlines are used the way we want.
- [ ] We know how to approve/decline people and guests, confirm over capacity, cancel an RSVP for someone who
      can't come, and check people in (Dashboard → Event → Attendees & check-in).
- [ ] Cancelling an event, or changing its time or place, sends everyone who RSVP'd an urgent in-app notification.
- [ ] We know **Email everyone going** (opens your email app, BCC) and the CSV export (no dietary data).
- [ ] Volunteer shifts (Dashboard → Volunteers) fit how we organize cooking, setup and clean-up.

## 2. Meals and allergens

- [ ] The cook enters **every ingredient** per dish — sauces, dressings, oils, toppings, packaged items — checking labels.
- [ ] We understand the three kinds of allergen information:
      **Contains** (from ingredients) · **May contain** (cross-contact the kitchen enters: shared equipment, fryers, boards) ·
      **Related** (e.g. sesame oil vs sesame seeds, wheat vs gluten).
- [ ] Sesame seeds and sesame oil are always entered **separately**.
- [ ] The general kitchen note (e.g. "shared kitchen that also handles sesame and tree nuts") is accurate.
- [ ] Staff tick the **preparation confirmation** only after checking it matches how the food is actually made.
      Menus can't be published without it.
- [ ] Published menus are frozen; changes go in a new version with a "what changed" note.
- [ ] Any allergen-relevant change — including day-of substitutions — gets an **urgent** update (notifies everyone who RSVP'd).
- [ ] The disclaimer wording and the contact details shown with every menu are right (Dashboard → Settings).

## 3. Voting

- [ ] Votes and suggestions are **advisory**; staff choose the menu.
- [ ] **A popular dish is never evidence it's safe for an allergy.** We never call food "safe", "allergen-free" or "guaranteed".

## 4. Privacy and roles

- [ ] Only **Kitchen staff** and **Admins** see names next to allergies (Event → Dietary), and every view is
      logged. Keep that group small. Organizers see anonymous counts.
- [ ] People can opt out of sharing with the kitchen (we'll ask them directly) and delete their dietary info
      or account anytime in Profile & settings. `/privacy` matches how we actually handle information.
- [ ] Lehigh addresses get *Student*, everyone else *Community guest*; the student domain list is right (Settings).
- [ ] At least **two** trusted admins. We know how to give and remove roles (People & roles).

## 5. Before launch

- [ ] Demo content removed (Dashboard → Getting started → **Remove demo content**).
- [ ] Contact details and parking (pre-filled) confirmed, the phone number added (Settings).
- [ ] Sign-in emails arrive at a Lehigh and a Gmail address.
- [ ] One dry run: create event → publish menu → RSVPs → check-in.
