Chabad at Lehigh

Shabbat dinners, events, menus and helping out — in one place

Free Phone or computer No password

A simple website for the Chabad House, with nothing to install. Public events, their menus and directions need no account. To RSVP, sign in with your email.

For students and guests

  • Shabbat dinner: tap Going or Not going. Change it until the RSVP deadline. Guests and notes are optional extras.
  • Events: a list or a month calendar, with filters such as Shabbat dinners. Add public events to Google or Apple Calendar.
  • Menus: once a menu is posted, each dish shows its ingredients and allergens. No RSVP needed to see them.
  • Save your allergies privately. If a posted dish contains or may contain one, you see an alert.
  • Help out: sign up to cook, set up or clean up, and see how many spots are left.
  • Vote and suggest dishes. These are ideas; the staff decide the menu.
  • Your activity: events, Shabbat dinners and times you helped.

For the people who run Chabad

  • One dashboard on a phone or a computer: events, RSVPs, approvals, the automatic waitlist and check-in at the door.
  • Duplicate last week's dinner in one tap. The menu comes along as a draft; volunteer shifts open when you publish.
  • Menus are published only after staff confirm the ingredients, allergens and cross-contact notes match how the food will be made.
  • Kitchen staff get a dietary report for each dinner, and every view is logged. Organizers see counts, not names.
  • Announcements, volunteer shifts, people and roles, and a "This semester" summary of events, attendance and new faces.
  • A built-in Help guide and a getting-started checklist.

Food allergies: what the app shows

  • Allergens come in three kinds, kept apart: ContainsMay contain (cross-contact)Related (e.g. sesame oil vs. sesame seeds)
  • The app never calls food "safe", "allergen-free" or "guaranteed". It shows what the kitchen entered, and every menu shows who to ask.
  • With a serious allergy, always talk to the organizers before the meal.

Privacy

  • Sign in with an email link or a code. There are no passwords.
  • Your dietary details are seen only by you and, for events you RSVP to, by kitchen staff and admins through a logged report. You can turn that sharing off.
  • Delete your dietary details or your whole account any time in Profile & settings.

Not launched yet. Built and tested; it launches only after the directors review it and hold a trial dinner. Lehigh University is not involved.
